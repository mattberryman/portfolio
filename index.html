Work

Reference tools built from the inside.

Three properties with one idea behind them: payments professionals should be able to reach the primary source, and so should their AI.

EU Payments Law paymentslaw.eu

EU payments law, built for people who have to use it. PSD2, PSD3, the PSR, FiDA, eIDAS2 and the AI Act, with the supporting acts around them, as cross-linked, searchable provisions rather than EUR-Lex PDFs. Follow an obligation from PSD2 into PSD3 and the PSR article by article, and hover a defined term for its in-force wording.

Free MCP server, no API key: ground Claude or ChatGPT in the corpus.

Open paymentslaw.eu

3DS Reference 3dsspec.com

A source-attributed reference for EMV 3-D Secure. Every data element and message type in the current Core specification, with formats, values, inclusion conditions and the page they came from. Build a required-field checklist for a channel and category, and compare reconstructed source cells across editions.

MCP for AI clients: messages, fields and protocol flows with citations.

Open 3dsspec.com

Transaction Intelligence transactionintelligence.net

Independent essays on payments, behaviour and the quiet plumbing of finance: the regulation as written, the systems as built, and the people in between. Long-form analysis of authentication, fraud, open banking and the PSD3 package, with a members' tier for the pieces that go further.

Where the analysis behind the reference tools gets written up.

About

Where I'm useful

  • Customer success and sales engineering leadership. Building and running global teams that turn technical depth into commercial outcomes for Tier 1 banks, processors and partners, from RFP through deployment to renewal.
  • Payment authentication. 3-D Secure, risk-based authentication and strong customer authentication as they are actually deployed: issuer and acquirer configuration, approval rates, friction and fraud exposure in the same conversation.
  • EU payments regulation. PSD2 to PSD3 and the PSR, DORA, eIDAS2 and the RTS on SCA, read as text with dates and articles rather than as a summary, and translated into what a product roadmap has to do about it.
  • Applied AI in regulated businesses. MCP servers, structured extraction and Claude-based tooling that teams use every day, built with the constraints in view: source attribution, versioning and knowing when the answer is not in the corpus.
